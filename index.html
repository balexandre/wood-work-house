Questions to ask before you sign

The Danish line under each one is ready to paste into your reply.

  1. Scaffolding in writing. You were told it's free, but the quote doesn't mention it.
    Vil I skrive ind i tilbuddet, at stillads/lift er inkluderet i prisen?
  2. 75 m of overhang and fascia. I estimate about 62–65 m from the plan. What else is included?
    Hvad er med i de ca. 75 løbende meter udhæng og stern?
  3. Horizontal boards on the gables. HardiePlank goes on horizontally, so the look changes from today's vertical boards.
    Monteres HardiePlank vandret på gavlene?
  4. The dark band above the brickwork. It isn't mentioned. Is it replaced or left as it is?
    Udskiftes det mørke bræt/den mørke beklædning over murværket, eller bliver det siddende?
  5. Gutter fittings. The new gutters hang in front of the fascia. The 66 konsoljern in the quote are the brackets that hold them there, about one every 56 cm, so check they're the kind that screw onto the fascia. Not listed: corner pieces for the two inner corners, downpipe clips, and more than 3 joiners for about 37 m of gutter.
    Er de 66 konsoljern til montering på sternen? Og er indvendige hjørner, rørholdere og nok samlestykker med i prisen?
  6. How many downpipes? Two black pipes run up the east gable into the soffit. If one is a downpipe, the house has 4 and the quote lists 3.
    Er der regnet med alle nedløb – også ved østgavlen?
  7. Working next to the asbestos roof. The roof sheets are asbestos cement and stay as they are, but the new fascia and gutters go on right at their edge, so the sheets mustn't be cut, drilled or broken. Also ask whether any of the old materials coming down contain asbestos. You take them away yourself, and asbestos has to be wrapped and handed in separately.
    Hvordan sikrer I, at tagpladerne ikke bliver skåret, boret eller knækket, når stern og tagrender monteres? Og indeholder noget af det nedrevne materiale asbest?
  8. Hidden damage and terms. The hourly rate if rot turns up, the payment plan, the start date, and whether AB-Forbruger applies.
    Hvad er timeprisen for ekstraarbejde ved råd? Arbejdes der efter AB-Forbruger?
